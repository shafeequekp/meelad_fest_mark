{% comment %} Reusable submit-button loader: prevents accidental double submissions by showing a spinner and guarding the form while it is being saved. - Native (non-AJAX) POST forms are handled automatically by the document level "submit" listener below. - Forms that manage their own loading (AJAX submits etc.) should add the data-no-loader attribute to opt out, or call MeeladLoader.form(this) / MeeladLoader.restore(this) manually in their JS. IMPORTANT: the submit button is visually "disabled" but NOT given the native `disabled` attribute. Setting `disabled` on the clicked submit button while the submit event is in flight makes browsers drop that button's name=value pair from the POST payload, so views that key on the submit button's name (e.g. `if "student_form" in request.POST`) would never fire and the form would silently "do nothing". We keep the spinner look and guard against double-submission with busy flags instead. {% endcomment %}
